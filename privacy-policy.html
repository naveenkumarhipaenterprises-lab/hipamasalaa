1. Commitment to Your Privacy

HIPA Enterprises ("HIPA Masalas", "we", "us", or "our") operates the e-commerce website located at hipamasalas.com and hipamasala-ecommerce.vercel.app. We are committed to safeguarding the privacy and digital security of our customers, website visitors, and trade partners in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act, 2023.

2. Information We Collect

We collect only the essential personal data necessary to provide seamless spice ordering, delivery, and support services:

  • Customer Contact Details: Full Name, Email Address, and 10-digit Mobile Phone Number.
  • Shipping & Invoicing Details: Delivery street address, apartment/flat number, landmark, city, state, and 6-digit postal PIN code.
  • B2B Commercial Details: Business name, business type (restaurant, distributor, caterer), estimated monthly procurement volume, and GSTIN (if provided for tax invoices).
  • Transaction Identifiers: Razorpay payment order IDs, payment mode chosen, and transaction timestamp. (Note: We never collect or store card numbers, PINs, or passwords).
  • Technical Usage Data: Anonymized browser type, device category, IP address, and cookie preferences to ensure optimal mobile display and prevent fraud.
Payment Security Guarantee: All payment transactions are encrypted via 256-bit TLS and processed by RBI-licensed payment aggregator Razorpay. HIPA Masalas does not have access to, nor do we ever store, your credit/debit card numbers, CVVs, or bank netbanking passwords.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your data solely for lawful and direct business operations:

  1. Order Fulfillment: Packaging your spice orders and generating courier waybills for accurate door delivery.
  2. Customer Notifications: Sending automated order confirmation messages, invoice receipts, and live courier tracking links via SMS, WhatsApp, or Email.
  3. Customer Support: Responding to order queries, address updates, or replacement requests.
  4. Accounting & Legal Compliance: Generating tax-compliant GST invoices and maintaining required audit records under Indian commercial law.
  5. B2B Follow-Up: Providing wholesale quotations and freight schedules to verified commercial buyers.

4. Data Sharing & Third-Party Disclosure

We do not sell, rent, trade, or monetize your personal data. Your personal information is disclosed only to verified service providers strictly necessary to execute your order:

  • Courier Partners (e.g., Delhivery, Bluedart, DTDC): Shared details are strictly limited to your delivery name, address, pincode, and contact number for delivery execution.
  • Payment Aggregator (Razorpay Software Pvt Ltd): For secure digital transaction authorization.
  • Statutory Authorities: If required by Indian law, court order, or official government regulatory audit.

5. Data Retention & Storage Security

Customer order records and tax invoices are maintained securely in encrypted data stores in accordance with statutory GST retention guidelines (typically 6-8 years). We apply stringent administrative, physical, and technical safeguards to prevent unauthorized data access, leakage, or loss.

6. Your Rights Regarding Your Data

As a valued customer, you have the right to:

  • Review and request a copy of the personal information stored in your account.
  • Request corrections to any outdated or inaccurate delivery addresses or phone numbers.
  • Request deletion of your non-mandatory account profile by emailing support@hipamasalas.com.

7. Updates to This Policy

We may occasionally update this Privacy Policy to reflect enhancements in our operational practices or regulatory changes. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated "Last Updated" timestamp.
