1. Food Safety & Nature of Consumable Goods

Because HIPA Masalas products consist of edible spice blends, ground spices, and consumable kitchen grocery ingredients, our returns policy is governed strictly by food safety regulations. Once a sealed spice packet leaves our temperature-controlled facility and arrives at a customer’s residence, it cannot be physically restocked or resold to another consumer.

Consequently, we do not accept general returns or exchanges for change of mind or personal taste preferences. However, we stand behind the quality and integrity of every pack we ship and provide prompt remedies in genuine cases of transit damage or fulfillment error.

2. Eligible Circumstances for Replacement or Refund

We will provide an immediate free replacement or a 100% refund under the following verified conditions:

  • Transit Damage: The outer shipping carton or inner foil pouch was torn, crushed, punctured, or leaked during courier transit.
  • Tampered / Broken Seal: The security seal or heat-sealed pouch seam arrived broken or defective.
  • Incorrect Product Dispatched: The item, pack size, or blend received does not match what was confirmed in your order invoice.
  • Manufacturing Defect or Contamination: An uncompromised factory pack displays any abnormal texture, foreign matter, or quality defect upon immediate opening.
  • Expired Product: Any product delivered with an expired shelf-life (Best Before date already elapsed upon receipt).
Quick Resolution Guarantee: Valid claims reported within 48 hours are reviewed by our operations manager within 4 business hours. If approved, replacements are prioritized for direct same-day or next-day dispatch.

3. Step-by-Step Claim Procedure (Within 48 Hours)

To initiate a damaged item claim or incorrect delivery replacement, follow these simple steps:

  1. Document the Issue: Take clear photographs or a short video showing:
    • The shipping carton and courier label showing your order number.
    • The specific pouch damage, broken seal, or incorrect item label.
    • The printed batch number and Best Before date on the back of the pouch.
  2. Contact Us Within 48 Hours: Send the details via:
    • WhatsApp: +91 70580 53055
    • Email: support@hipamasalas.com with subject "Damage Claim - Order #[Your Order Number]"
  3. Claim Verification: Our customer support desk will evaluate the photos and confirm your preferred solution (Replacement or Refund) within 4 to 12 business hours.

4. Refund Processing & Timelines

If a monetary refund is requested and approved:

  • Refunds are processed back to the original payment method used during checkout through our RBI-licensed gateway partner, Razorpay.
  • UPI & Netbanking payments: Usually credited back to your bank account within 24 to 48 hours.
  • Credit & Debit Cards: Reflected on your bank statement within 5 to 7 business days, depending on your card issuer's billing cycle.
  • You will receive an official Razorpay refund confirmation ARN (Application Reference Number) to track the reversal with your bank.

5. Non-Eligible Situations

Please note that claims cannot be honored under the following circumstances:

  • Claims submitted more than 48 hours after courier delivery confirmation.
  • Products that have been partially or substantially consumed prior to claim notification.
  • Improper storage after delivery (e.g., storing open packs in wet or humid areas, causing clumping).
  • Packages returned or undelivered due to an incorrect shipping address, wrong pincode, or recipient unavailability after 3 courier attempts.

6. Order Cancellation Policy

You may request cancellation of an online order anytime before it has been dispatched from our facility.

  • To cancel, contact our customer desk immediately at +91 70580 53055 or via WhatsApp with your order number.
  • Once dispatched and an AWB tracking number has been generated, orders cannot be intercepted or cancelled in transit.
  • Approved pre-dispatch cancellations receive a 100% instant full refund.
