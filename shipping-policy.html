1. Direct Factory Dispatch & Freshness Guarantee

At HIPA Masalas, we operate on a fresh-batch grinding and packaging schedule. To ensure you receive spice blends with maximum essential oil retention and natural aroma, our retail and commercial packs are dispatched directly from our packaging unit in Chennai, Tamil Nadu.

Order Minimum Requirement: HIPA Masalas online store fulfills orders with a minimum cart value of ₹999. This minimum threshold ensures we can package every spice box safely and ship directly via premium express couriers.

2. Order Processing & Dispatch Timelines

Every order placed on our official online store is verified, safely packed, and dispatched according to the following schedule:

  • Order Confirmation: Instant confirmation via SMS and Email upon successful payment verification.
  • Dispatch Window: All orders are dispatched within 24 to 48 business hours (excluding Sundays and national holidays).
  • Batch Quality Check: Before sealing inside corrugated shipping master boxes, pouch seals and batch dates are inspected manually.

3. Delivery Timeframes by Region

We partner with leading surface and air logistics providers (including Delhivery, Bluedart, DTDC, and India Post) across India. Standard transit times from our Chennai dispatch center are:

  • Tamil Nadu & South India (Metros & Major Cities): 2 to 4 business days
  • Rest of India (Tier 1 & Tier 2 Cities): 4 to 6 business days
  • Remote Regions / Northeast / Jammu & Kashmir / Andaman & Nicobar: 6 to 9 business days

Please note: During festive seasons, severe weather advisories, or state-wide holidays, logistics partners may experience minor transit delays beyond our direct control. We continuously track parcels and assist our customers until safe delivery.

4. Shipping Charges & Free Delivery

We believe in honest and transparent pricing:

  • Orders Meeting ₹999 Minimum Value: We offer FREE Standard Pan-India Shipping on retail consumer orders meeting our minimum order threshold.
  • B2B & Bulk Supply Consignments: Commercial orders (> 25 kg) placed through our B2B quotation desk are shipped via dedicated surface transport or regional logistics with freight charges quoted transparently at actuals based on weight and destination.

5. Tamper-Evident Packaging Standards

Spices and ground masalas are sensitive to air, humidity, and heat. To preserve the fresh aroma of coriander, red chillies, and roasted cumin:

  1. Individual spice powders are sealed inside multi-layer, food-grade metalized moisture barrier pouches.
  2. Individual packs are securely wrapped and cushioned inside heavy-duty 3-ply corrugated cartons.
  3. Master cartons are sealed with tamper-evident security tape. If the outer tape or carton appears torn, wet, or opened upon delivery, please document photographs and contact our support immediately.

6. Order Tracking & Real-Time Notifications

Once your shipment is handed over to the courier partner, an automated dispatch notification is sent to your registered phone number (via SMS/WhatsApp) and email address. The notification includes:

  • Assigned courier partner name (e.g., Delhivery, Bluedart)
  • Waybill / Consignment Tracking Number (AWB)
  • Direct live tracking hyperlink to follow parcel movements in real-time

7. Non-Delivery & Failed Delivery Attempts

Our delivery partners make up to 3 delivery attempts before returning a parcel to our facility. Please ensure your shipping address, pincode, and mobile phone number are entered accurately during checkout. If a parcel is returned due to incorrect address details or refusal to accept without valid cause, re-shipping charges may apply.

Parcel Inspection Advice: If you receive an outer package that is visibly damaged, leaking, or crushed in transit, please take 2–3 photos before opening and notify us within 48 hours for immediate replacement under our Return & Refund Policy.
